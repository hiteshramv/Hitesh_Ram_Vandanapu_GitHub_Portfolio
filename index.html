Get to Know Me

I’m Hitesh, a Perception Engineer currently based in Ingolstadt, Germany. My engineering journey began with a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering in India and gradually led me toward Digital Engineering, computer vision, and robotics. Moving from a mechanical engineering background into perception has given me an appreciation for both the physical world and the algorithms that allow machines to understand it.

During my Master's studies, I became particularly interested in computer vision and 3D perception, and I started working with real-world sensor data from LiDAR and cameras. This interest eventually developed into my work on multi-sensor perception and tracking, where I explored how different sensors can complement each other to build a more reliable understanding of the environment.

Today, my main areas of interest are 3D perception, sensor fusion, multi-object tracking, state estimation, and real-time computer vision. I enjoy working on problems where theory meets practical implementation, especially when there are challenges around noisy sensor data, coordinate transformations, computational efficiency, and real-world performance.

What I enjoy most about engineering is taking a problem that initially seems complex, breaking it down into smaller pieces, understanding what is happening underneath, and gradually turning it into a working system. I like learning by building, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding why something works rather than simply making it work.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy travelling, photography, and binge-watching. Travelling gives me an opportunity to explore new places and experience different environments, while photography has made me more observant of details, composition, and the world around me. I also enjoy learning new technologies and working on small projects whenever I come across an idea that interests me.

I’m currently interested in opportunities where I can work on challenging problems in computer vision, perception, sensor fusion, and robotics, while continuing to grow as an engineer and contribute to systems that have a real-world impact.
